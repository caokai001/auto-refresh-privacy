页面自动刷新：Microsoft Edge 隐私说明

页面自动刷新不会向开发者收集、出售或发送浏览历史、网页内容、账号凭据或使用统计。

刷新间隔与缓存偏好通过 Microsoft Edge 的同步存储保存，启用浏览器同步时可能随浏览器账号同步。语言偏好仅保存在本机。运行中的任务将标签页编号和设置保存在浏览器会话存储中，重启浏览器后清除。

刷新标签页会像正常刷新一样访问该网站。打开爱发电或通过微信支付时，将使用第三方服务，并适用其条款和隐私政策。本扩展不处理支付信息。

Auto Refresh for Microsoft Edge: Privacy Policy

Auto Refresh does not collect, sell or send browsing history, page contents, account credentials or analytics to the developer.

Your interval and cache preference use Microsoft Edge sync storage and may sync through your browser account when browser synchronization is enabled. Your language preference is stored locally. Active timers store tab IDs and settings in browser session storage and are cleared when the browser restarts.

Reloading a tab contacts that website just like a normal refresh. Opening Afdian or paying through WeChat uses a third-party service governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Payment details are not handled by this extension.
